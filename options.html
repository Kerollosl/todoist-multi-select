Todoist Task Toolkit

The hand-off sends only the sorting instructions and your Todoist project link to ChatGPT, using the page you are signed in to. The extension does not read your tasks for this and has no servers. ChatGPT, the Todoist connector and your plan can change, in which case the draft is left for you to review and send. Nothing is sent without the final checks passing, and it never completes or deletes tasks itself.

Shortcuts: change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (copy the address into a new tab).
